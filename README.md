## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm an aviation maintenance planning and technical operations professional with a **BEng (Hons) in Aerospace Systems Engineering** and over four years of industry experience coordinating **Base, Line and Aircraft on Ground (AOG) maintenance inputs across a diverse range of aircraft types, including Bombardier Global and Challenger platforms, among others**.

My background spans maintenance planning, AOG coordination, technical documentation, work-pack preparation, customer liaison, resource coordination and the commercial aspects of aircraft maintenance operations.

Working in time-critical maintenance environments has given me a practical understanding of the **operational and commercial pressures faced by aircraft owners, operators, MROs and aviation service providers**. I recognise the importance of minimising aircraft downtime, controlling costs, coordinating resources and meeting demanding schedules, without compromising safety, regulatory compliance or airworthiness.

Alongside my aviation career, I'm pursuing an **MSc in Computer Science with Software Engineering**, developing skills in Python, data analytics, algorithms and software development.

My objective is to combine **aviation industry experience, engineering knowledge and modern computational techniques** to develop practical solutions to real-world operational and commercial challenges.
